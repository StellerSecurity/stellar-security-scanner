Reproducing the exact wrapper review

No wrapper or Gradle distribution is executed. Use only your existing reviewed
JDK; this tool never installs or downloads one. The helper verifies classes with
initialization disabled and resolves declared types only. It never invokes a
wrapper method or constructor. javap reads classfile instructions as data.

Example:
python3 -B tools/review_gradle_wrapper_bytecode.py \
  --java-home '/Applications/Android Studio.app/Contents/jbr/Contents/Home' \
  --jar /absolute/path/to/gradle-wrapper.jar \
  --output /private/tmp/wrapper-static-review

Only the two independently reviewed whole-JAR hashes are accepted. Compare the
full disassembly, metadata and recorded security-sensitive flows with
reviews/gradle-wrapper-bytecode.json. Passing structural verification is not an
automated semantic safety verdict, execution approval, general JAR exemption,
Gradle distribution review or advisory override. The runtime/distribution and
Gradle dependency-graph gaps must be resolved separately.

Any changed archive byte or containing member path loses recognition. Constants
still receive normal malware inspection. Unknown/nested JARs, standalone
classes, malformed classes, native members and changed metadata remain blocked
or incomplete. Regression tests exercise those boundaries.
